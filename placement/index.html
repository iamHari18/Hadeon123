How core electronics hiring works

The exact process differs by company, but most fresher recruitments for core roles follow the same sequence. A company may merge rounds (technical and HR together) or add one (a coding test, a group discussion, or a take-home assignment).

StageFormatTypical durationWhat it checksCommon reason people drop out
1. Eligibility screenCGPA cut-off, branch, backlogs—Basic criteria set by the companyActive backlogs, CGPA below cut-off
2. Online testAptitude + technical MCQs, sometimes C or coding60–120 minSpeed and accuracy on fundamentalsRunning out of time; negative marking
3. Technical interview 1One-to-one or panel30–60 minCore subject fundamentals, problem solving on paperKnowing definitions but not reasons
4. Technical interview 2Senior engineer or manager30–60 minProject depth, design thinking, how you debugNot being able to explain own project
5. HR interviewOne-to-one15–30 minCommunication, honesty, relocation, bond termsMemorised answers, unclear motivation
6. OfferLetter, sometimes after document verification———

Where most candidates are filteredThe online test removes the largest number of applicants. Practising timed aptitude and technical MCQs gives more return per hour than anything else in the first weeks.

8-week preparation roadmap

Assumes about 2–3 hours a day alongside classes. Aptitude runs every day in parallel because it is a speed skill. Shift weeks 5–6 towards whichever domain matches your target roles.

Before the interview

  • Résumé: one page, projects first, every line something you can explain for five minutes.
  • Project: a block diagram you can draw on paper, the key numbers, and one problem you solved.
  • Fundamentals: one page of formulas and definitions per domain, revised the night before.
  • Company: what it makes, its products or customers, and what the team you are applying to does.
  • Documents: mark sheets, ID, printed résumé, passport photos, and offer-letter terms if you already have one.
  • Questions for them: two prepared questions about the work and training.

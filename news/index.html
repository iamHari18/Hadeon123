Electronics industry news

The most important electronics and semiconductor stories, updated every day and explained in plain words. Each story links to its original source. Where a story connects naturally to a subject you study, that link is noted.

7 November 2026

  • 3 Nov

    Global chip sales hit $517.7 billion in the third quarter

    The Semiconductor Industry Association reported worldwide chip sales of $517.7 billion for July–September 2026, up 24.1% from the previous quarter. September alone came to $172.6 billion, well over double September 2025, driven largely by demand for AI hardware.

    Industry · Source: Semiconductor Industry Association

  • 6 Nov

    Nvidia reportedly asks TSMC to make 3D DRAM

    According to reports, Nvidia has asked TSMC, the world's largest contract chipmaker, to manufacture advanced 3D DRAM, which stacks memory cells vertically. If TSMC agrees, it would compete with memory makers such as Samsung and Micron. Neither company has officially confirmed the plan.

    Syllabus link: a DRAM cell is one transistor plus one capacitor. The MOS capacitor in Solid State Devices (Module 3) explains how that charge is stored.

    Chips and tools · Source: NewsBytes

  • 5 Nov

    Intel's weak outlook and the memory shortage hit chip stocks

    Intel forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $15–16 billion, below analyst expectations. It blamed weaker PC demand and a shortage of memory chips that is raising costs, a shortage it expects to last at least into the first half of 2027. Chip stocks fell sharply that day amid wider worries about AI spending, then partly recovered on 6 November.

    Industry · Source: Yahoo Finance

  • 5 Nov

    US chip industry could be short of about 157,000 workers by 2030

    The industry group SEMI warns of a large gap in technicians and engineers as new US fabs come up. Community colleges are launching short programs: one in Arizona trains students in 12 weeks using VR and university cleanrooms. TSMC's Arizona project alone is expected to create around 25,000 jobs.

    Why it matters: fab process, device physics and test skills are in demand worldwide, including at India's new plants.

    Hiring · Source: KPBS

  • 3–4 Nov

    Delhi summit focuses on India's place in the chip supply chain

    The ISIG Executive Summit in New Delhi brought together the India Semiconductor Mission, the Semiconductor Laboratory (SCL) and companies including Infineon, Nexperia, Mitsubishi Electric and Navitas. Topics included advanced packaging, OSAT (assembly and test) and wide-bandgap power devices made from silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN).

    India · Source: ANI

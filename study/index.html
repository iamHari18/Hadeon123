Study material for electronics subjects

Notes for the core ECE subjects, written for both university exams and interviews. They are being added one subject at a time. Until a subject's notes are ready, the table links to related interview questions that already cover part of it.

"Coming soon" means the notes are not published yet. This page will be updated when each one goes live, and the change will be listed on the updates page.

How the notes will be written

  • Each topic starts with a plain-language explanation before the formal theory.
  • Worked numerical examples with every step shown.
  • A short list of interview questions on that topic, linked to the question banks.
  • A one-page revision sheet per module.

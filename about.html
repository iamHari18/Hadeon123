About Hadeon

Hadeon is a free placement-preparation and study site for students heading into core electronics jobs.

Preparing for core-electronics placements meant collecting questions and answers from many places, and most of them were either too shallow, IT-focused, or wrong in small ways. Hadeon is an attempt to put the important questions in one place, answered properly.

Accuracy and corrections

Answers are written from standard textbook theory and checked, but mistakes are possible. If you find one, open an issue on the site's GitHub repository with the page and question number, and it will be corrected.

Independent project

Hadeon is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any company, university or exam body.
